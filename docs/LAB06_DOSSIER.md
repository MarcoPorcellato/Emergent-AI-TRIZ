# Lab 06 — one-principle causal pilot dossier

The machine-readable dossier at
`experiments/lab06-causal-intervention/dossier.json` is intentionally
`blocked_by_h1`. It prepares one future causal pilot without selecting an
operator from an observed causal result and without authorizing any model,
target, training, or material run.

## Selection rule

The operator is selected only after three independent TRIZ experts close H1 and
Lab 05 demonstrates an out-of-sample held-out-domain direction. The selection
must be frozen before causal output is inspected. Segmentation and Inversion
are candidate IDs, not current labels or validated constructs.

## Frozen intervention surface

The eventual single pilot must include positive and opposite-sign steering,
ablation, three dose levels, norm-matched random directions, unrelated-label
directions, a no-intervention baseline, and fluency/capability-preservation
tasks. The primary outcome is held-out-domain operator behavior. Recognition,
source-exposed competence, and generic helpfulness cannot rescue a failed
causal primary.

## Approval boundary

All model, target, training, and run authorization flags remain false. Before a
material run, a new exact dossier must bind the selected operator, canonical
labels, control receipts, model/runtime identity, statistical plan, resource
ceiling, external dense-asset policy, CCP admission, and one-attempt rule.
Every terminal class (`positive`, `null`, `failed`, `non_interpretable`) must be
published, and no positive result may be promoted to a general TRIZ claim.
